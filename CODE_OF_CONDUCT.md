# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in issues, reviews, discussions and project events deserves respectful treatment. Describe reproducible behaviour, explain technical disagreement, and give contributors room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, unwanted contact and disclosure of private messages or device information are unacceptable. Do not post someone else's logs or captures without their permission.

Report incidents to the repository owner through GitHub's private reporting or moderation tools. Include a link to the relevant interaction and any context you are comfortable sharing. Maintainers will limit disclosure, hear affected participants when appropriate and may remove content, close discussions or restrict access. Participants can ask the owner to reconsider a moderation decision privately.
